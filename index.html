The problem

Finding meaningful opportunities is harder than it should be.

Most students are told to volunteer, join clubs, and find internships — but given no real guidance on where to actually start.

📋

Too many random lists

Students waste time searching through outdated or irrelevant opportunities with no sense of what actually fits them.

🤷

Hard to know what fits

Most platforms don't explain which activities match a student's goals, grade, schedule, or interests — they just list everything.

📦

Growth gets forgotten

Students rarely track what they did, what they learned, or how their experiences shaped them over time.

How it works

How CommonGround works

1

Track your growth

Why it matters

Built to make opportunity discovery more accessible.

The students who find the best opportunities early are usually the ones with more guidance. CommonGround changes that.

Levels the playing field

Students without private counselors or professional networks get the same personalized guidance as anyone else.

Reduces overwhelm

Instead of endless searching, students get a short ranked list of matches that actually make sense for them.

Connects interests to action

CommonGround bridges the gap between "I'm interested in medicine" and "here's exactly what you can do about it."

Builds self-awareness over time

Tracking experiences and reflections helps students understand who they are — not just what they did.

Supports students who don't know where to start

Designed specifically for students who feel behind or lost — not just those who already have a plan.
